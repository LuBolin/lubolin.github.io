## Definitions
What is Interaction Design (IxD)?
Interaction goes both ways – not just one.
- Designing behavior of products/systems when interacted with by humans
- Designing behavior of humans when interacting with by products/systems
- Designing interactions between:
  - human and product/system
  - Remote humans (mediated by a product/system)
  - Co-located humans (mediated by a product/system)
- Design to connect and enable


What is mobile?
Mobile is NOT:
- Just about mobile phones
- Just about mobile phone apps

Mobile is:
- Small: fits into the pocket preferably, carried on you
- Portable: does not have to be plugged in or attended to regularly
- Connected: connected to networks of some sort
- Interactive: able to accept input and provide output in some form
- Contextually aware:
  - understands what it is attached or connected to
  - able to gather information about context
  - able to get things done within that context
